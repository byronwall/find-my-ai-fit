## Purpose

This is a personalized, take-home prompt library for attendees of the Indiana AI Roadshow in Indianapolis. It collects the prompts demonstrated during the workshop and adds a practical library organized around common small- and mid-market-business workloads. The prompts are presented as tool-neutral: attendees can use them in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, or Big Impact Group's browser-based AI Studio. Bracketed text marks information the user should customize.

## Content summary

The page contains five main sections:

1. **Prompting best practices** — Five side-by-side plain and upgraded prompt examples covering explicit calculations, golden examples, asking clarifying questions, iterative second drafts, and task chaining.
2. **The Perfect Prompt Creator** — A reusable meta-prompt that interviews the user and iteratively improves a prompt through a Prompt/Critique/Questions cycle.
3. **AI Use Case Generator** — A long workshop-assistant prompt that uses the BADGE framework (Build, Augment, Decide, Guard, Engage) to generate, document, and compile practical AI use cases.
4. **Demo prompt library** — Four prompts used in live demonstrations: a reusable business profile, a thought-partner ladder, a BLAH-filter use-case interview, and a multi-step workflow with a human approval checkpoint.
5. **Take-home library by business area** — Twenty-three prompts grouped into Every Business, Sales & Business Development, Marketing & Customer Growth, People & Operations, and Leadership.

The business library covers meeting notes, email drafting, document summarization, plain-language rewriting, SOPs, customer complaints, vendor comparisons, proposals, quote follow-ups, cold outreach, market research, overdue invoices, content calendars, review responses, local-listing copy, expertise-driven content, hiring, onboarding, difficult conversations, policy drafting, weekly updates, decision briefs, and 90-day action planning.

## Safety and operating guidance

The site emphasizes two rules:

1. Do not enter sensitive customer, financial, health, personal, NDA-covered, or unreleased product/IP information. De-identify information or use fictional stand-ins before pasting it into an AI tool.
2. Keep a human in the loop. AI produces drafts; the user reviews and decides before anything is sent, published, or finalized.

The page also recommends professional review for policies or decisions involving legal or financial consequences and encourages users to verify current web research against cited sources.

## Audience and tone

The content is aimed at business owners, managers, and functional teams that want low-risk, immediately useful AI workflows. The writing is practical and plainspoken, with a strong focus on work that can save time during the current week rather than large transformation programs or custom software projects.
